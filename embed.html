About Me

I am a Ph.D.-qualified engineer with 8+ years of combined industry, research, and academic experience spanning machine vision, control systems, power electronics, instrumentation, and renewable energy.

As Team Lead – Machine Vision at Manatec Electronics, I lead a team developing vision-based inspection and measurement solutions. Earlier, as Lead – Product Development at Lectronic Solutions, I designed instrumentation hardware including a tank water-level controller and a pressure transmitter with a Windows calibration app. My doctoral research developed a nonlinear controller for DC-link voltage stabilization in DFIG-based wind turbines, validated from 0.75 kW hardware to 2 MW simulation.
